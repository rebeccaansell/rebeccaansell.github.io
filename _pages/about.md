I'm a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at Georgetown University. My research focuses on fine-tuning large language models, perceived humanness of AI-generated content, reasoning in LLMs, and computational social science applications.

I work on LLM fine-tuning, AI-generated content detection, and computational social science, with a focus on perceived humanness in political discourse. I am also involved in the [Public Debt is Public Project](https://publicdebtispublic.mdi.georgetown.edu/), mentor [MDI Scholars](https://mdi.georgetown.edu/mdi-scholars/), and work as a computer science tutor. I am advised by [Prof. Lisa O. Singh](https://gufaculty360.georgetown.edu/s/contact/00336000014RlCVAA0/lisa-o-singh-phd).
