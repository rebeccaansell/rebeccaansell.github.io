In their research article, "Analyzing the perceived humanness of AI-generated social media content around the presidential debate," Dr. Tiago Ventura, Rebecca Ansell, Dr. Sejin Paik, Autumn Toney, and Prof. Leticia Bode investigate how the public perceives AI-generated content in the context of the 2024 U.S. presidential debate.

The study reveals that while individuals can generally distinguish between human-authored and AI-generated posts, the platform significantly influences this perception. The findings suggest that users are more likely to identify AI content on X (formerly Twitter) compared to YouTube, highlighting the nuances of how different platforms shape user interactions with political content.

This research sheds light on the complexities of AI's role in political discourse and emphasizes the importance of understanding audience perceptions in an era of increasing AI integration.
